      How to explore the graph

      Each dot is a subject; each line is an existing relationship. Select a subject to see its prerequisites, related pages and connections. Use the focused view to explore its immediate neighbourhood.

      Prerequisites come from the existing learning maps; the quantum map also includes suggested study order. Page links are references within teaching content. The selected subject panel shows the direction of each relationship.

      Drag the background to pan, drag a dot to move it, and scroll to zoom. With the graph focused, use the arrow keys to pan, + and − to zoom, Enter to open the selected subject and Escape to clear the selection. You can also use the buttons in the subject list.
